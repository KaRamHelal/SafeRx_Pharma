## [3.1.0] - 2026-10-28

Additive: no field was removed and no request that worked before is refused. One field's
**meaning** changed (see Changed).

### Changed
- **`winner.confidence` on a prescription line is now reading certainty.** It says how
  sure SafeRx is that the line was *read* as written: the agreement of two independent
  reads of the line, field by field (name, dose, unit), with the weakest field governing.
  1 means both reads agree; a field only one read saw, or a line read once, is 0.5; a dose
  or unit the reads disagree on is 0. Below 0.9 the line always goes to a person.
  - Before 3.1.0 this field carried the registry **match** score. That is `final_score`,
    which is unchanged.
  - If you thresholded on `winner.confidence`, you were thresholding on the match. Use
    `review_required` to decide whether a person must look; use `final_score` for match
    quality and `confidence` for reading quality.
  - `ocr_candidates[]` now lists every distinct reading of the line (primary first), with
    its script in `locale` and the line's reading certainty in `confidence`.
- **Every prescription line is resolved under the same rules as `POST /registry/resolve`**,
  with each independent reading of the line as evidence. The readings must agree: a second
  reading that names a different medicine or product withdraws the selection
  (`ocr_readings_disagree`), and a line that touches a known look-alike pair
  (hydroxyzine/hydralazine, for example) is never selected (`lookalike_risk`).
- **Resolution decides by evidence.** A product that contradicts anything written
  (strength on the full set, such as 875/125; dosage form; route, including "im" or
  "syrup" in the text; salt; release type) is never selected. Unspecified release means
  immediate release. A misspelled name is offered, never selected.

### Added
- **Prescription outcomes are stated** in `reason_code`:

  | Outcome | `status` | `reason_code` | Billed |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Medicines read | `completed` / `awaiting_review` | `null` | yes |
  | Not a prescription | `completed` | `not_a_prescription` | no |
  | A prescription with no readable medicine (review required) | `completed` | `no_medications_read` | no |
  | The OCR provider failed; nothing was read | `failed` | `ocr_provider_unavailable` | no |
  | Review completed with lines nobody resolved | `completed` | `review_incomplete` | yes |

  A failed read is never reported as "no medicines". `document_context.medications_present`
  is `true` only when medicines were read and is never inferred as `false`.
- **`POST /ocr/prescriptions/{id}/review` takes decisions** (`PrescriptionReviewRequest`):
  `decisions[]` of `{line_id, action, sfrx_id}` with `action` `select_candidate`,
  `enter_sfrx_id` or `not_a_medicine`, and `complete`. When no line still needs a person,
  or `complete` is true, the safety check runs and the prescription becomes `completed`.
  An empty body still only reads the state. New errors: `409 prescription_review_not_open`,
  `409 prescription_review_conflict`, `422 review.line_unknown`, `422 review.line_duplicated`,
  `422 review.candidate_not_offered`, `422 review.sfrx_id_unknown`,
  `422 request.sfrx_id_invalid`, `503 dependency_unavailable` (decisions saved; post again).
  New status `safety_incomplete`: decisions were saved but the safety check after them could
  not run.
- **Each prescription line explains itself.** `review_reason_codes` says why the line needs
  a person (resolution codes plus `ocr_reading_uncertain`, `ocr_judge_override`,
  `ocr_line_unreadable`). `informational_reason_codes` reports
  `equivalent_product_selected` when the clinical identity is certain and one of several
  brands was chosen; it never blocks the safety check. No line is dropped: a line with no
  registry match stays, `review_state: unresolved`.
- **Alternatives are truthful.** `selection_reason: same_generic_name` is another product of
  the selected medicine. `ocr_alternative_match` is a different medicine whose name is close
  to what was read, never an equivalent.
- **New resolution reason codes**, after which nothing is selected and the item's
  `candidates` are the medicines to choose from: `strength_not_available`,
  `form_not_available`, `route_not_available`, `salt_not_available`,
  `release_not_available`, `approximate_name_match`, `ocr_readings_disagree` and
  `lookalike_risk`. Treat every one of them like `ambiguous_candidates`. In a safety check
  they make the medication `ambiguous`, with candidates and a `reason` in words.
- Request bounds on `POST /registry/resolve` are documented: more than 50 medications is
  `422 request.collection_too_large`, and a malformed `sfrx_id` is
  `422 request.sfrx_id_invalid`. A well-formed id no product carries stays the item outcome
  `sfrx_id_unknown`.

### Fixed
- **`equivalent_candidates[]` is a list of objects** (`sfrx_id`, `registry_concept_id`,
  `display_name`, `equivalence_reason`). 3.0.1 declared it as a list of strings, so a
  client validating against the closed schema rejected production responses that list the
  same product in other pack sizes.
- `candidate_medications[]` declares `review_reason_codes` and `informational_reason_codes`,
  which production returns; a 3.0.1 closed-schema client rejected them.
- `enterprise_ocr_prescription_resolve` and `enterprise_ocr_prescription_safety` are
  documented as what they are: they report the resolution and safety state that the
  workflow produces on its own, and do not run either step again.
- `selected_candidate_id` is documented as the selected candidate's `candidate_id`, not its
  `sfrx_id`; the resolve example used the wrong one.

## [3.0.1] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- **Plans are published.** Free, Starter, Growth, Scale and Custom, with prices, daily
  allowances and per-key request rates
  ([Limits and retries](/authentication/rate-limits#plans)).
  - Every plan grants every capability; tiers differ only in volume and rate.
  - A plan can run for a fixed term (an annual term or a complimentary upgrade) and then
    revert on its end date. Your keys follow the plan in force without being re-issued.

- Registry suggest/search items gain optional `primary_name` (the brand) and `package`
  (the pack description).

### Changed
- **Product names follow one rule:** `{name and strength} - {dosage form} - {manufacturer}`,
  e.g. `KLACID 250 - Oral Tablet - KAHIRA`, or `WARFARIN 2 - Oral Tablet - Sanofi`, or in Arabic
  `وارفارين 2 - أقراص - سانوفى`.
  - Names no longer repeat the generic (`WARFARIN 2 WARFARIN Oral Tablet` before) and never show
    the raw `A|B` separator.
  - The ingredients are in the separate `generic` field (`A + B` for a combination).

### Fixed
- The Python and C# clients send a named `User-Agent` (`saferx-pharma-python/<version>`,
  `saferx-pharma-csharp/<version>`). .NET's `HttpClient` sends none by default, and the
  SafeRx edge refused requests without one before they reached the API.
- Docs: you issue, rotate and revoke your own keys in the Partner Portal. Earlier text said
  SafeRx handled them on request.

No request or response schema changed.

## [3.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Changed (breaking)
- **The safety-check result is redesigned** (`EnterpriseSafetyCheckResult`, `schema_version: 2`)
  for `POST /safety/checks`, `POST /erx/safety-checks`, and the `result` of
  `GET /safety/checks/{check_id}` and of batch items. Every field is built from a stated
  source. Each text is exactly `{en, ar}`, and a value the sources do not state is `null`.

  | Before (2.x) | Now (3.0.0) |
  |---|---|
  | `signals[]` | `findings[]`, with `kind`, `severity`, `action`, `title`, `explanation`, `recommendation`, `medication_refs`, `subjects`, `evidence`, `confidence`, `based_on_context` and `review_required` |
  | `domain_coverage[]` and `unavailable_domains[]` | `domains[]`: each requested domain once, `status` (adds `reference_only`), `reason`, `missing_context` and `release_id` |
  | `resolved_medications[]` | `medications[]`: `ref`, `role` (`new`/`current`), `status`, `selection`, `product` and `candidates` |
  | `context_summary` | `patient_context` (`status`, `applied_fields`) |
  | `risk_display` | `findings[].evidence.source_rating`, the database's own rating |
  | `summary`, `limitations`, `correction_anchors`, `educational_context`, `source_provenance`, `display_obligations`, `result_id`, `surface`, `locale` | replaced by `outcome`, `review.reasons`, `release` and `disclaimer`; `check_id` identifies the result |
  | `confidence` always a number | the source's own confidence, or `null` |

- **Severity bands follow each database's own labels.** Food and allergy findings were one band
  too severe.
- **DDI release V8.3.24** rates anticoagulant + aspirin and anticoagulant + NSAID as Major
  (previously Contraindicated).
- **The 422 `enterprise.safety.target_resolution_required` body carries `medications[]`**, so
  candidates are structured instead of embedded in the text.
- **A check stored in the 2.x format returns `410 enterprise.safety.stored_check_format_retired`.**

## [2.1.1] - 2026-10-26

## [2.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Allergy reference** (included in plans with safety checks):
  - `GET /allergies/families` lists every allergy family with English and Arabic names,
    clinical significance, reviewed aliases and cross-reactive families (0 units).
  - `GET /allergies/substances?q=` is a prefix search, in English and Arabic, over families,
    single-component ingredients and products that carry allergy data (1 `drug_lookups`
    unit). Ingredients are named by an opaque `substance_id`.
  - `POST /allergies/resolve` resolves up to 50 allergies by text, `family_id`, `sfrx_id` or
    `substance_id` (1 `safety_boards` unit).
  - Every result carries a `patient_context_entry` you can pass unchanged as one
    `patient_context.allergies[]` entry of a safety check; it resolves to the same families
    there.
- **Stored checks**: every `POST /safety/checks` and `POST /erx/safety-checks` response
  carries a `check_id`. `GET /safety/checks/{check_id}` returns the stored inputs and result
  for 90 days, to any key of your organization (0 units). Other organizations get `404`.
- **Asynchronous batches**: `POST /safety/check-batches` takes up to 100 items. It returns
  `202` with one `check_id` per item; poll each with `GET /safety/checks/{check_id}`
  (`pending`, then `completed` or `failed`). A batch costs 1 `safety_boards` unit per
  evaluated item, and nothing for a failed item.
- **Request size limits**: JSON bodies are capped at 2 MiB (`413 request.body_too_large`).
  Collections are capped at 100 batch items and at 50 medications, allergy-resolve items or
  registry-resolve items (`422 request.collection_too_large`). Oversized requests are
  refused before anything is charged.

### Fixed
- An allergy given only as text in `patient_context.allergies` (for example
  `{"input_text": "amoxicillin"}`) now produces allergy findings. Previously Enterprise
  checks matched allergies only by explicit family or product identifiers.
- Common class words now resolve to their allergy family: "penicillin", "statins",
  "PPI", "بنسلين" and 79 more reviewed aliases (allergy release V1.2.27).

### Removed
- Nothing is deferred any more. The five operations formerly marked `x-status: deferred` are
  available and included in the SDKs.

## [2.0.5] - 2026-10-21

### Added
- `enterprise_erx_safety_check` has a working request contract. `erx_payload` is a structured
  prescription: `prescription_ref`, `lines[]` (1-50; `line_ref`, `input_text` and/or `sfrx_id`,
  `dose_text`/`route`/`dosage_form` to pick the product, `frequency_text`/`duration_text`
  echoed only), `current_medications[]` and `patient_context`. `requested_domains` is optional.
  The response is a `SafetyCheckResponse` with an `erx` echo of the prescription, line by line.
  Until this release no documented eRx body could succeed: the previous `erx_payload` was an
  empty closed object the backend refused.

### Changed
- Self-serve plans (Free, Trial) have a short-term per-key ceiling in addition to the daily
  quota. A trip returns `429 quota.exceeded` with `quota.window: "hour"`. Paid plans are
  unaffected. The rate-limits page now also states that daily quota is a rolling 24-hour
  window, not a fixed calendar day.

### Fixed
- `SafetyCheckResponse` `signals[].risk_display.raw_source_label` is nullable; production
  returns `null` when the source carries no native label.
- The five deferred operations declare the `503 capability_unavailable` they return to an
  entitled caller, beside the `403 API_KEY_NOT_ENTITLED` every other caller receives.
- Enterprise OCR responses now match the published `PrescriptionCreated` and `PrescriptionResult`
  schemas (no extra fields; complete `educational_context`). `enterprise_ocr_prescription_read`,
  `resolve`, `safety` and `review` no longer fail with a gateway 502, and
  `enterprise_ocr_prescription_events` returns `text/event-stream` as documented. A backend
  4xx on eRx or OCR now reaches the caller with its real status instead of `503
  trusted_accounting_finalization_unavailable`. (Server-side fixes; no SDK change.)
- Quota now works as documented (server-side, 2026-10-22). Each of the five classes
  (`drug_lookups`, `autocomplete`, `safety_boards`, `ocr_prescriptions`, `mcp_calls`) is one
  shared daily allowance, and a quota error names the class in `quota.metric`. A safety or eRx
  check costs 1 unit (it cost 2), and eRx checks share the `safety_boards` pool. OCR follow-up
  calls and MCP protocol requests that run no tool cost 0, and a request SafeRx refuses
  (`4xx`) costs nothing. Before this fix each operation had its own counter and every call was
  charged an extra unit. Registry lookups are now capped at the published daily allowance.

## [2.0.4] - 2026-10-21

A support ticket could not run a DDI check at all: plain names returned
`422 target_resolution_required`, and `sfrx_id` values returned `503`. The
backend now resolves the way customers write medicines; this release carries
the contract and client side.

### Added
- **Medications may be given by `sfrx_id` alone.** `input_text` is optional
  when `sfrx_id` is present on `enterprise_safety_check` and
  `enterprise_registry_resolve` (and the hosted MCP tools). An explicit
  `sfrx_id` is authoritative and an unknown one is reported as
  `sfrx_id_unknown`, never replaced by a text match.
- **`dose_text`, `route` and `dosage_form` on safety-check medications.** They
  select the registry product (strength, route, form) and are not read by the
  safety evaluation. Inline qualifiers in `input_text` ("Warfarin 5 mg tablet",
  "Augmentin 1g", Arabic units) are parsed the same way.
- **A bare name runs the check.** "Warfarin" or "Aspirin" is evaluated as its
  default systemic variant with `review.required: true`,
  `default_variant_selected` (or `equivalent_product_selected`) in the reason
  codes, and a limitation naming the product and how to pin it.
- **`resolved_medications[]`** on `SafetyCheckResponse`: which registry product
  each request medication was evaluated as, with `resolution_status` and
  `review_reason_codes` (table in the Safety results concept page).

- **`enterprise_capabilities` and `enterprise_status` are available.** Both were
  published as deferred while production had been serving them to every entitled
  key; they are promoted with their real schemas (`EnterpriseCapabilities`,
  `EnterpriseStatus`), SDK methods and example fixtures, live-verified on
  2026-10-21. 16 operations are now available, 5 remain deferred.
- **Idempotent replay.** A retry with the same `Idempotency-Key` and body returns
  the original response with `X-SafeRx-Idempotent-Replay: true`; the same key with
  a different body is `409 quota.idempotency.conflict` (Rate limits page).
- **`SafetyCheckResponse` declares every field production returns**:
  `context_summary.context_sources` / `profile_context_version`,
  `display_obligations.approved_summary_template_id`, per-domain and per-signal
  `correction_anchors`, `SafetySignal.risk_display` / `title_origin` /
  `patient_context_attribution`, `LocalizedString.ar_normalized` / `eg_ar` /
  `source_locale`, and `SourceProvenance.model_release_id` / `resolver_version` /
  `search_artifact_id`. A strict client built to 2.0.3 rejected real responses.

### Fixed
- A retry with the same `Idempotency-Key` returned
  `503 trusted_accounting_finalization_unavailable` on every idempotency-governed
  operation (safety check, e-Rx check, OCR create), so the SDKs' retry loops never
  recovered from a transient failure. Server-side defect, fixed 2026-10-21.
- The hosted MCP tools `check_medication_safety` and `resolve_medications`
  answered every real call with `isError: "SafeRx rejected an invalid upstream
  response"`. Server-side defect in output validation, fixed 2026-10-21.
- Deferred operations denied inconsistently: four returned `503
  capability_unavailable` to callers without the bundle, one returned the
  documented `403 API_KEY_NOT_ENTITLED`. Every route now authenticates and checks
  entitlement first; `503 capability_unavailable` is reserved for an entitled
  caller of a disabled capability (Authentication overview, "Denial order").
- Internal per-domain adapter telemetry (`adapter_id`, `adapter_version`,
  `adapter_health_status`, `adapter_limitations`, `missing_context_reasons`,
  `schema_version`, `source_version`, `domain_release_id`) leaked on every
  `domain_coverage` item. Removed server-side; the published item schema was
  already right.
- `frequency_text` was advertised on safety-check medications but the backend
  refused it with `422`; it is removed from the contract and the MCP server
  schema. The backend now names the refused field in `request_invalid`.
- A one-character or over-long `input_text` next to other medications no longer
  fails the whole request (it was a `503 dependency_unavailable`); that entry is
  `unresolved` with `query_rejected` and the rest are evaluated.

## [2.0.3] - 2026-09-23

Outside-developer blackbox pass (published 2.0.2 packages, public repository
and docs.saferx.online only, real signed calls against production) found the
defects below. Backend corrections shipped in SafeRx production the same day;
this release carries the client and contract side.

### Fixed
- **Python and TypeScript clients reused the first attempt's nonce, timestamp,
  and signature on every retry.** The API rejects a reused nonce as a replay,
  so any transient `429`/`502`/`503`/`504` followed by the client's own retry
  ended in a hard denial instead of the retried result. Both clients now sign
  every attempt afresh (same `X-Request-ID` and `Idempotency-Key`, new nonce).
  The C# client already did this. Regression-tested behaviorally.
- **The Postman collection could never sign a request**: its pre-request
  script declared `query` twice (a syntax error, so it never ran) and signed
  an undefined `path`. It also targeted `/status` and `/capabilities`, two
  deferred operations no key can reach. Rewritten to a working
  `saferx-hmac-sha256-v1` implementation covering registry search,
  autocomplete, resolve, safety capabilities, and a safety check.
- `LocalizedString.en`/`.ar` no longer require a non-empty string: real
  registry records carry an empty string where no text exists in that
  language (for example `route.ar` on many products), so a strict validator
  rejected every live search, autocomplete, and product-detail response.
- `SourceProvenance` documents `arabic_content_snapshot_id`, present on every
  registry response. `model_release_id`, `resolver_version`, and
  `search_artifact_id` (pipeline identity the schema description already
  excluded) are now stripped server-side instead of leaking.
- `RegistryProductDetail`: documented `pharmacology.mechanism`,
  `pharmacology.summary`, `prose_details.directions_en`, and
  `warnings.warnings_en`, all returned live and previously undeclared; the
  internal `warnings.safety_check_actions` and the resolve echo's
  `query.source` are stripped server-side.
- `ProblemDetails.code` now documents the signed-request denial codes. Until
  this release every authentication or authorization failure was a single
  `403 API_KEY_DENIED`, so a wrong signature, a replayed nonce, and a missing
  entitlement were indistinguishable; the API now returns `401` with
  `API_KEY_INVALID` / `API_KEY_SIGNATURE_INVALID` / `API_KEY_TIMESTAMP_INVALID`
  / `API_KEY_NONCE_REJECTED`, and `403` with `API_KEY_NOT_ENTITLED` /
  `API_KEY_EXPIRED` / `API_KEY_DENIED`. The authentication guide carries the
  table.
- MCP guide: a key without `mcp_standard` is denied at the gateway for every
  JSON-RPC method (there is no filtered `tools/list`), and the hosted MCP
  endpoint no longer leaks `policy_id` in its denials.
- Developer access guide: links the actual self-service signup page and states
  that `enterprise_free` grants every documented surface (registry, safety,
  eRx, OCR, hosted MCP). Self-serve and admin-issued keys had been issued
  with registry-only route access because the plan catalog named entitlement
  bundles that did not exist; keys now derive their access from the
  organization's plan at validation time.
- Key rotation and revocation are described as handled by SafeRx on request;
  the Partner portal key console is not yet self-service, and the previous
  wording told developers to use it.
- `enterprise_capabilities`, `enterprise_status`, `enterprise_safety_check_read`,
  `enterprise_safety_check_batch`, `enterprise_allergy_resolve`,
  `enterprise_allergy_families`, and `enterprise_allergy_substances` were
  documented as returning `501` once reached. No backend implements them and
  no plan grants them, so every caller gets `403` (now `API_KEY_NOT_ENTITLED`).
  Corrected the documented response for all seven.
- Registry search/autocomplete/product-detail responses had carried
  `correction_anchors` and three internal Arabic-normalization keys on every
  `LocalizedString`; product detail also carried 14 undeclared fields. All
  stripped server-side (deployed 2026-09-14; confirmed absent in this pass).

### Added
- `RegistryProductSummaryList` and `RegistrySuggestionList` items document
  `manufacturer`, `product_tier`, `name_strength`, `match_reason`, `score`,
  and (for suggestions) `generic`/`strength`/`supplier`/`pharma_uses`/
  structured `match`.
- `MedicationResolution.candidates` rebuilt to the real shape
  (`candidate_id`, `confidence`, `display_name`, `normalized_name`,
  `dosage_form`, `route`, `manufacturer`, `strength`, `ingredient_names`,
  `match_reasons`, `equivalent_candidates`, `registry_concept_id`, `review`,
  `source_provenance`).

### Changed
- `scripts/release_preflight.py` fails if `release/current.yaml`'s
  `release_version` matches an already-tagged (`vX.Y.Z`) release whose
  `openapi/enterprise-v1.yaml`, `openapi/components.yaml`, or
  `release/current.yaml` content differs from what is checked in: a corrected
  contract always ships under a new version.
