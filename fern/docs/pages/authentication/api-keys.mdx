---
title: Enterprise keys
subtitle: Credential handling
---

Your Enterprise key is both your account identifier and your HMAC signing
secret — see [Authentication](/authentication/overview) for the exact
signing contract. There is no separate signing key to manage.

Keys for the existing `enterprise_free` plan are self-service (see
[Developer access](/authentication/developer-portal)); larger plans are
admin-assigned. Either way, the key is shown exactly once at issuance and
cannot be retrieved again — only rotated.

Store the key in your organization's secret manager, inject it at runtime,
and rotate it through your Partner account when needed. Do not place it in
a browser bundle, Postman collection, repository, issue, or support
message — see [Server-side-only use](/security/overview) for why a
browser-embedded key is a real exposure, not a theoretical one.
