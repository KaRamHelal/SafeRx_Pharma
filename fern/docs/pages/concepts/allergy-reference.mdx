---
title: Allergy reference
subtitle: Not yet available
---

`enterprise_allergy_resolve`, `enterprise_allergy_families`, and
`enterprise_allergy_substances` are documented in the
[API reference](/api-reference) for contract completeness, but none of them
have a live success response yet — each currently returns `501`. They will
be documented here as usable operations once they have a real, evidence-backed
success contract.

**Allergy state can still be considered today** via
`patient_context.allergy_state` and `patient_context.allergies` on
[`enterprise_safety_check`](/concepts/safety-results) — the dedicated
allergy-reference lookup operations above are a separate, not-yet-available
capability from allergy-aware safety checking.
