---
title: Prescription review
subtitle: Completing a prescription that waits for a person
---

A prescription whose `status` is `awaiting_review` has at least one line that
SafeRx would not decide on its own: the reads disagreed, the line touches a
look-alike pair, no product has what was read, or nothing legible was read
(see [OCR prescription lifecycle](/concepts/ocr-lifecycle)). Those lines have
`review_required: true` and say why in `review_reason_codes`. Lines read with
certainty are already resolved and need nothing from you.

`POST /ocr/prescriptions/{prescription_id}/review`
(`enterprise_ocr_prescription_review`) takes the reviewer's decisions and
completes the prescription.

## Decisions

```json
{
  "decisions": [
    {"line_id": "line_0002", "action": "select_candidate", "sfrx_id": "SRX-101-000311"},
    {"line_id": "line_0003", "action": "enter_sfrx_id", "sfrx_id": "SRX-204-001876"},
    {"line_id": "line_0004", "action": "not_a_medicine"}
  ],
  "complete": false
}
```

| `action` | Meaning | `sfrx_id` |
|---|---|---|
| `select_candidate` | The product is the line's `winner` or one of its `alternatives` | required, one the line offered |
| `enter_sfrx_id` | Any active registry product, for example one found with `enterprise_registry_resolve` or `enterprise_registry_search` | required, verified against the registry |
| `not_a_medicine` | The line is not a medicine (a note, a signature, a header); it is excluded from the safety check | ignored |

At most one decision per line and 50 decisions per request. You can send
decisions in several requests: each request saves its decisions.

## Completion

The review **completes** when no line still needs a person, or when you send
`"complete": true`:

1. the safety check runs over the resolved and reviewed lines;
2. the prescription becomes `completed` (`awaiting_review` → `safety_running` →
   `completed`);
3. each decided line shows `review_state: reviewed` (or `not_a_medicine`) and
   the chosen product as its `winner`. `winner.confidence` keeps the measured
   reading certainty — the person chose the product; the reading did not become
   more certain.

If you complete with lines still undecided, those lines stay
`review_required: true`, the safety check covers only the other lines, and the
result carries `reason_code: review_incomplete` with the prescription's review
still required. **That is never a clean result**: an unchecked line may
interact with the others.

If decisions leave lines undecided and `complete` is not set, the decisions are
saved and the prescription stays `awaiting_review`.

An empty body (or no body) only returns the current state, as before 3.1.0.

## Errors

| Status | `code` | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 409 | `prescription_review_not_open` | The prescription is not awaiting review (it is still being read, or already completed) |
| 409 | `prescription_review_conflict` | Another request changed the prescription at the same time. Read it and retry |
| 422 | `invalid_request` | The body does not match `PrescriptionReviewRequest` |
| 422 | `review.line_unknown` | No such `line_id` on this prescription |
| 422 | `review.line_duplicated` | More than one decision for one line |
| 422 | `request.sfrx_id_invalid` | `sfrx_id` is missing or not `SRX-NNN-NNNNNN` |
| 422 | `review.candidate_not_offered` | `select_candidate` named a product the line did not offer; use `enter_sfrx_id` |
| 422 | `review.sfrx_id_unknown` | `enter_sfrx_id` named no active registry product |
| 503 | `dependency_unavailable` | The product could not be verified, or the safety check could not run. Your decisions are saved; post again after `Retry-After` |
| 404 | `NOT_FOUND` | No such prescription for your organization |

A `422` applies none of the request's decisions: every decision is checked
before any is saved. After a `409 prescription_review_conflict` or a `503`, read
the prescription to see what was saved before posting again.

When the safety check after a review cannot run, the prescription moves to
`safety_incomplete`. Post the review again (an empty decisions list with
`"complete": true` is enough) to retry the check.
