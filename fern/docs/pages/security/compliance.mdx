The public contract is allowlist-based and closed-schema: every response
schema in this documentation is exactly what you'll receive, with
`additionalProperties: false` enforced. It excludes credentials, private
service topology, internal storage identifiers, and person-specific
routing or accounting fields. See [Security overview](/security/overview)
for what that means for logging on your side.

## What SafeRx provides

- A closed, versioned response contract (`schema_version` on every response).
- `educational_context` and `display_obligations` on every response,
  establishing that results are pharmaceutical education, not medical
  advice or a dispensing authorization.
- `review` state and `source_provenance` so you can trace and gate on
  professional-review requirements.

## What remains your responsibility

- **Access control** — who in your organization/application can trigger a
  safety check or view a result.
- **Retention** — how long you keep request/response data, and where.
  SafeRx does not dictate your retention policy; align it with your own
  regulatory obligations.
- **Professional review workflows** — enforcing `review.required` and
  `review.minimum_reviewer_role` in your own application logic before
  relying on a result. The API tells you review is required; it doesn't
  enforce that your integration honors it.
- **Audit** — logging your own access and decisions, without capturing raw
  patient data or credentials (see [Prohibited logging](/security/overview)).

This API is a bounded, closed-schema medical-education reference contract.
It is not a substitute for your own compliance program, and using it does
not by itself satisfy any regulatory requirement your application is
subject to.
