title: Developer access
subtitle: Self-service signup for the existing free plan
---

Enterprise access for the existing self-service **`enterprise_free`** plan
is self-service: open the Partner portal signup at
[saferx.online/partner-portal/self-serve/signup](https://saferx.online/partner-portal/self-serve/signup),
sign in with a verified identity, then complete signup. No manual approval
step is required for the free plan.

Your organization is provisioned on `enterprise_free` and issued an API key
immediately on signup. **The key is shown exactly once at issuance** — store
it securely right away; it cannot be retrieved again later, only rotated.
Signup is limited to one free-tier organization per verified identity.

`enterprise_free` grants every documented surface: registry lookup, safety
checks, eRx safety, prescription OCR, and the hosted MCP server, within the
allowances in [Limits and retries](/authentication/rate-limits).

Larger plans (Starter, Growth, Scale, Custom) are admin-assigned rather than
self-service today — contact SafeRx to discuss those. Key rotation and
revocation are currently handled by SafeRx on request; the Partner portal
key console is not yet self-service.

Use the exact base URL and operation IDs in this documentation once your key
is issued. See [Authentication overview](/authentication/overview) for exact
request signing and [Limits and retries](/authentication/rate-limits) for
`enterprise_free`'s quota allowances.
