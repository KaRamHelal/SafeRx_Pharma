title: Allergy reference
subtitle: Families, substances and resolution over the active allergy release
---

Three operations read the active allergy release. They're included in every plan that
includes safety checks.

| Operation | What it does | Units |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /allergies/families` | Every allergy family: English and Arabic names, clinical significance, reviewed aliases and cross-reactive families, with severity 1-5 | 0 |
| `GET /allergies/substances?q=amox` | Prefix search, in English or Arabic, over families, single-component ingredients and products that carry allergy data | 1 `drug_lookups` |
| `POST /allergies/resolve` | Resolves up to 50 declared allergies to their families | 1 `safety_boards` |

## From a declared allergy to a safety check

1. Find the allergen. Search with `GET /allergies/substances?q=...`; use `kind=family`,
   `ingredient` or `product` to narrow it. Or resolve what the patient told you directly
   with `POST /allergies/resolve`, by `input_text`, `family_id`, `sfrx_id` or a
   `substance_id` from search.
2. Pass the result's `patient_context_entry` unchanged as one
   `patient_context.allergies[]` entry of a safety check, with
   `"allergy_state": "has_entries"`.

```json
{
  "locale": "en",
  "requested_domains": ["allergy"],
  "medications": [{"input_text": "Augmentin"}],
  "patient_context": {
    "allergy_state": "has_entries",
    "allergies": [{"input_text": "amoxicillin"}]
  }
}
```

The check resolves the entry to exactly the families the resolve call reported. The two
calls use one index, so they can't disagree.

## How matching works

- **Resolution is exact** after normalization: case, Arabic letter variants and
  diacritics, punctuation.
  - A family name or one of its reviewed aliases ("penicillin", "statins", "PPI", "بنسلين")
    wins first.
  - Then an ingredient's own name, then a product's exact tradename.
  - A tradename shared by products with different families comes back as `ambiguous`.
  - Use the substances search for partial names, then pass the identifier it returns.
- **Ingredients keep their own families.** A combination product's families are never
  attributed to each of its components. For example, trimethoprim is not reported as a
  sulfonamide because it is sold combined with sulfamethoxazole.
- **No DrugBank identifiers are returned.** Ingredients are named by an opaque, stable
  `substance_id` that resolve accepts.
- **Search is bounded.** `q` must be 2-80 characters, `limit` is at most 20, and `cursor`
  stops 100 results deep. There is no empty-query browse.

Results are pharmaceutical reference data from the release, never patient data, and they
carry the same educational context, provenance and display obligations as every safety
response.
