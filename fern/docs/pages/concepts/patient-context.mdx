---
title: Patient context
subtitle: Request-scoped patient factors for safety evaluation
---

`patient_context` on [`enterprise_safety_check`](/api-reference) (and the
hosted MCP's `check_medication_safety`) lets you supply patient factors that
sharpen safety evaluation beyond a bare medication list. It's optional on
every request.

<Warning>
Enterprise has no saved profile. There is nothing to opt into or out of —
every field you want considered must be present on the request itself. A
field omitted this time is not remembered from a previous call, and is not
inferred from account history.
</Warning>

## Omitted means unknown, not "no risk"

If you don't send `pregnancy_status`, the evaluator treats it as unknown —
not as "not pregnant." The same applies to every field. Send `unknown`
explicitly (where the field supports it, like `pregnancy_status` and
`allergy_state`) when you've asked the patient and they don't know, rather
than omitting the field, since some evaluators distinguish "asked and
unknown" from "never asked."

## Age

Two ways to express age, and they behave differently over time:

- **`date_of_birth`** (preferred) — the server derives current age at
  evaluation time. Send this once; it stays correct on every future request
  without updating anything.
- **`age_years` + `age_reference_date`** — a snapshot age as of a specific
  date, for when you only have "the patient is 34" without a birth date.
  Age is advanced from `age_reference_date` server-side, so a stale snapshot
  still produces a roughly-correct current age rather than a frozen one.

## Pregnancy and lactation

`pregnancy_status` / `lactation_status` take `pregnant`/`lactating`,
`not_pregnant`/`not_lactating`, `unknown`, or `not_applicable`.
`not_applicable` and `unknown` are deliberately distinct — use
`not_applicable` when the status genuinely doesn't apply (e.g. `sex: male`),
and `unknown` when it could apply but you don't know.

For pregnancy detail, `pregnancy_context.lmp_date` is preferred; if you only
have a gestational-age snapshot, pair `gestational_age_weeks` (or
`_months_approx`) with `gestational_age_reference_date` so the server can
advance gestational age correctly over time, the same pattern as age. Do not
compute trimester client-side and send only that — send the underlying dates
or weeks and let the server derive it.

For lactation detail, `lactation_context` follows the same shape with infant
age instead of gestational age.

## Renal and hepatic function

Prefer numeric values (`renal.crcl_ml_min` or `renal.egfr_ml_min_1_73m2`;
`hepatic.child_pugh`) when you have them. Fall back to the categorical
`status` field (`normal`/`mild`/`moderate`/`severe`/...) when you only have a
clinical impression rather than a lab value.

## Conditions and allergies

`conditions` is a closed list of normalized clinical-dose condition
identifiers (see the request schema for the current set) — free text isn't
accepted here.

`allergy_state` distinguishes `unknown` from `confirmed_none` — always send
one explicitly when you know it, since "no entries in `allergies`" alone is
ambiguous between "not asked" and "asked, no allergies." When
`allergy_state` is `has_entries`, populate `allergies[]` with free-text
entries; `entry_type` tags each as `free_text`, `family`, or `product` to
help matching.

## Diet, supplements, and fasting

`diet_profile` covers diet patterns, fasting patterns, and specific
food/supplement exposures — relevant to the `food_interactions` and
`meal_timing` safety domains. Currently source-backed diet patterns are
`vegan`, `keto_low_carb`, `high_protein_fitness`, `mediterranean`,
`intermittent_fasting`, and `ramadan_fasting`; others are accepted as
non-signaling context. Individual exposures (`food_exposures`,
`supplement_exposures`) let you report specific items (e.g. grapefruit,
creatine) with a confidence level (`confirmed_current`, `frequent`,
`occasional`, `avoids`, `unknown`) rather than a blanket pattern.

## Reading it back

The response's `context_summary` tells you whether any of this was actually
used: `status: request_context_applied` means at least one `patient_context`
field was supplied and used; `baseline_only` means none was, so only
medication-level evaluation ran. `missing_context_count`,
`stale_context_count`, and `contradiction_count` flag gaps and problems —
see [Safety results](/concepts/safety-results) for the full response shape.
`context_summary` never echoes your raw `patient_context` values back.
