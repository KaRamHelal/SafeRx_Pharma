---
title: Enterprise keys
subtitle: Credential handling
---

Your Enterprise key is both your account identifier and your HMAC signing
secret — see [Authentication](/authentication/overview) for the exact
signing contract. There is no separate signing key to manage.

You issue keys yourself in the Partner Portal (see
[Developer access](/authentication/developer-portal)) on any plan. A key is
shown exactly once at issuance and cannot be retrieved again — only rotated.

A key carries your organization's current plan. When the plan changes, or a
fixed-term plan reaches its end date, every key you already hold follows it
on the next request; there is nothing to re-issue. A key can be limited to
fewer capabilities than your plan grants, never more.

Store the key in your organization's secret manager and inject it at
runtime. Rotate or revoke it in the Partner Portal; the old key is refused
from that moment. Do not place it in
a browser bundle, Postman collection, repository, issue, or support
message — see [Server-side-only use](/security/overview) for why a
browser-embedded key is a real exposure, not a theoretical one.
